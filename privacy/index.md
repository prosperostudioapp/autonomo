# Privacy policy — Autónomo Copiloto

_Last updated: 16 September 2026._

## The short version

Autónomo Copiloto keeps everything you enter on your phone. It has no
network access, no account, no analytics and no advertising. Nothing you
type is sent to the developer or to anyone else unless you yourself share
a file.

## What the app stores, and where

All of it stays in the app's private storage on this device:

- **Fiscal profile**: the IAE epígrafe, the tax regime, the activity start
  date and the answers you give in the wizard (VAT, SII, withholdings, the
  349/347 and similar) and, only if you fill them in, your NIF, name and
  municipality, used to prefill the borrador PDFs.
- **Ledger**: monthly income and expense totals, their IVA, and any note.
- **RETA base history** and the recorded modelo 130 / 303 filings.
- **Settings**: language, reminder preferences, the deductions you have
  ticked, the Verifactu questionnaire answers, and whether you accepted
  the legal notice.

Also in private storage, for a while: the copy of your data made before a
restore, kept until you put it back or discard it; and the temporary
files (a PDF or an export file you shared) in the app's cache.

The Android cloud backup (the copy Google makes of app data to your
Google account) is switched off for this app, so this data does not leave
the device that way.

## When data leaves the device — only when you choose

- **Export**: "Export data" in Settings builds a JSON file and opens the
  system share sheet. Where it goes is your choice (a file manager, mail,
  a cloud drive). The file is not encrypted.
- **Borrador PDFs**: "Share PDF" opens the same share sheet with the draft
  form, which carries the figures and the declarant boxes.
- **Safety-net copy (optional, Android 10+)**: if you turn it on in
  Settings, the app writes `Documents/AutonomoCopiloto/autonomo_backup.json`
  to shared storage every time you leave the app, so the data survives
  removing the app. That file is not encrypted and is visible to any app
  you give file access to. Turning the switch off deletes it.
- **Moving to a new phone (Android 12+)**: when you set up a new Android
  phone and choose to copy your apps from the old one, by cable or Wi‑Fi,
  the system copies this app's data directly from one phone to the other.
  It does not pass through a server. The copy kept for undoing a restore
  is not moved.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to show the deadline and base-change reminders you
  turn on. All reminders are scheduled on the phone.
- **Run at boot**: to reschedule those reminders after the phone restarts.

No internet permission is requested, so the app cannot transmit anything.

## Deleting your data

- "Reset profile" in Settings deletes the fiscal profile, including the
  NIF, name and municipality. The ledger, the RETA history, the recorded
  filings and the settings stay until you remove the app.
- Removing the app deletes everything in its private storage: the
  database, the settings, the undo copy and the temporary files.
- The optional safety-net copy in Documents is deleted by turning the
  switch off, or by deleting the file with the phone's Files app. A copy
  left by an earlier install can only be removed with the Files app.
- Files you exported or shared, and the data copied to a new phone, are
  under your control, wherever they are.

No server holds any of your data, so there is nothing to request from
the developer.

## Children

The app is aimed at self-employed adults in Spain and is not directed at
children.
